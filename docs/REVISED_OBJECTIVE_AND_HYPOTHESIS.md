# Revised objective, hypothesis, and study framing

## Objective

To characterize and compare peri-implant CBCT gray-value distributions at standardized sampling
locations around titanium implants in matched scan configurations acquired with and without
alginate, and to assess consistency across three trials and tray status.

## Hypotheses

**Null hypothesis:** Matched alginate and no-alginate scans have the same peri-implant gray-value
distribution under the tested acquisition conditions.

**Research hypothesis:** Matched alginate and no-alginate scans have different peri-implant
gray-value distributions. Direction and magnitude should be estimated from the data rather than
presupposed.

## Interpretation boundary

Peri-implant gray values are a protocol-dependent surrogate imaging outcome. A difference in their
distribution does not, by itself, demonstrate that metal artifact was reduced or worsened. A direct,
validated artifact metric and independent acquisition-level replication would be required for that
claim.

The study remains focused on metal-associated effects in CBCT. The workbook contains no optical
scan, dimensional-accuracy, or material-shrinkage outcome, so those interpretations are excluded.
